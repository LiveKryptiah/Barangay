ONLINE CLEARANCES

Official Barangay Documents.

Submit requirements online to expedite release upon claiming at the Barangay Hall.

₱50.00 Standard

Barangay Clearance.

Official record certification required for local employment, postal ID, police clearance, and bank accounts.

  • 1 Valid Government-Issued ID
  • Proof of Residency or Community Tax Certificate (Cedula)
  • Same-Day Processing upon physical verification
Free / Statutory Waived

Certificate of Indigency.

Free public welfare certification for hospital bills, medical subsidies, scholarship grants, and legal aid.

  • 1 Valid Government or Student ID
  • Clinical / Hospital bill or Scholarship referral letter
  • Statutory Free under Republic Act No. 11261
₱50.00 Standard

Certificate of Residency.

Affidavit of bonafide address for Meralco/water utility connections, school enrolment, or COMELEC transfer.

  • 1 Valid ID with local address
  • Proof of Billing or Lease Contract / Homeowners Certificate
  • 24-Hour Expedited Release
₱500.00 Commercial

Barangay Business Clearance.

Mandatory commercial clearance for DTI / SEC registered enterprises, stores, food establishments, and offices.

  • DTI Business Name / SEC Registration Certificate
  • Contract of Lease or Property Title of Commercial Site
  • Sanitary & BFP Safety Inspection endorsement
